Delete Your Account

Last updated: 9 September 2026

You can permanently delete your Zebal account and all associated data directly from within the app — no need to contact support.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Zebal app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the "Data & Cloud Backup" section.
  4. Tap "Delete My Account Permanently".
  5. Confirm when prompted. Your account and data are deleted immediately.

If you only want to clear the data stored on this device, without deleting your account, use "Erase All Local App Data" in the same Settings section instead.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email/phone, gender, date of birth, UPI ID)
  • Your personal expense records, budgets and bucket goals
  • Receipt photos you have scanned or uploaded
  • Your sign-in credentials (Google/Phone link, linked email/password)

What may be retained

Data you shared into a group may remain visible to that group, because it forms part of a shared record the other members rely on. We may also retain records we are required to keep to comply with applicable law.

Timeframe

Deletion takes effect immediately in the app. The underlying data is fully removed from our servers within 90 days.
